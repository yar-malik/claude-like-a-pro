# The 7-part brief

Copy this, fill in the brackets, paste it into Claude Code.

```
Goal: [what you want] for [who], so that [why]. Success = [a real number].
Quality bar: [vivid words: what it should feel like, what it must never look like].
Tools: [files, folders, sites or apps it can use].
Freedom: you choose [layout / order / words], and change the plan if you find a better one.
Check: review it three times against [your brand files] before you stop, and fix anything off.
Deliver: save it as [path / file name].
In one line: [the goal again, in one sentence].
```

## The one we used in the video

```
Goal: a one-page website for Pine Street Dog Walks that gets 10 new trial walks a month from neighbours.
Quality bar: it should feel like a friendly local cafe's site. Warm, real, nothing that looks like a template, a big chunky headline, one clear button.
Tools: use my files in brand/ for voice, design, audience and prices, and find any free fonts or icons you need.
Freedom: you choose the layout, sections and words, and change the plan if you find a better one.
Check: open it and review it three times against brand/ before you stop, and fix anything off-brand.
Deliver: save it as site/index.html.
In one line: a warm local site that makes people text for a free first walk. Work on your own and don't ask me anything until it's done.
```

The one-line version ("make me a website for a dog walking business") invented "insured", "background-checked" and
five-star reviews from customers who don't exist. This one used the real prices and the real dogs.
