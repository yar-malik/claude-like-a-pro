# Weekly post

One Instagram post a week, Sunday evening: a little story from the week, plus the free walk line.

## 1. Read (fresh every time, never from memory)

- `notes/week.md`: this week's notes. If it's empty or still last week's, stop and say so.
- `brand/voice.md`: how Sam writes.
- `brand/rules.md`: what every post must and must not do.
- `brand/offer.md`: the free first walk line and phone number. The only source for prices.
- `brand/story.md`: which dogs to feature, and what stays off social.
- `brand/audience.md`: the worry underneath (is my dog safe and happy while I'm not there?).

## 2. Pick one story

Pick ONE moment from the notes, not a recap of the whole week. Prefer one that:
- has a named dog in it (Biscuit gets the most love on Instagram),
- quietly answers the owner's worry: the dog is safe, happy, looked after,
- has a real detail from the notes (the 20 minutes, the covered path, the full loop).

Don't invent details that aren't in the notes. If you need one, ask.

## 3. Write the caption

Follow `brand/voice.md` exactly. In short:
- Short sentences. A neighbour talking, not a company.
- Use the dog's name. One joke at most. One exclamation mark at most.
- "Walk", never "adventure". Never "furry friends" or "fur babies".
- 3 to 6 short lines of story.
- Then ONE short question to the reader, tied to the story (e.g. "How's your old dog doing these days?"). Always. It goes on its own line, right before the free walk line.
- Then the free first walk line from `brand/offer.md`, copied with the current number.
- A few local hashtags at most, on the last line.

Must not:
- mention any price unless the notes ask for it, and then only exactly as in `brand/offer.md`;
- say "insured" or "certified";
- tell the Juniper story (that's for the About page only);
- offer anything listed under "Not offered".

## 4. Photo note

Say which photo to use: a real photo of that dog, outside. If Sam doesn't have it yet, write `[PHOTO: ...]` describing the shot. Never suggest stock or cartoon images. Remind: no house numbers visible.

## 5. Check, save, show

Before showing it, check the caption line by line against `brand/rules.md` and `brand/voice.md` (count the exclamation marks, check the number against offer.md, and check there's a question to the reader just before the free walk line).

Save to `posts/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly.md` (the Sunday's date) with: the caption, the photo note, and one alternative opening line. Show Sam the caption in chat.

Never post or schedule it. Sam posts it.
