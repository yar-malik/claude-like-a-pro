everything about my business (brain dump, sorry it's messy)

ok so the business is Pine Street Dog Walks. it's me, Sam, and sometimes my cousin Priya covers Fridays. we walk dogs on Pine Street and the four streets around it (Maple, Cedar, Holloway, and the bit of Grant Ave up to the park). I don't want to go wider than that, the whole point is I'm local and I can be there in 5 min.

how I talk: short sentences. like a neighbour, not a company. I hate "furry friends" and "fur babies", never ever use those. I always use the dog's name if I know it. one joke max, people want to know their dog is ok, not a comedy show. I say "walk" not "adventure". I never use exclamation marks more than once in a message. I sign texts "Sam" not "the team".

prices (these change sometimes so keep them in ONE place):
- solo walk, 30 min: $25
- pack walk, 60 min, max 4 dogs: $35
- puppy check-in, 15 min: $15
- first walk is free, always. you just text 555-0142
- 10% off if you book 5 walks in a week
- I don't do overnight sitting. people ask all the time. the answer is no, I recommend Rosa on Cedar for that

customers: mostly 3 kinds.
1. office people who leave at 8 and get back at 6. they feel guilty. they want a photo.
2. new parents. tired. the dog is suddenly getting less attention and they know it.
3. older owners whose knees can't do the long loop anymore. they want someone they can trust with a key.
everyone has the same question underneath: is my dog safe and happy while I'm not there?

look / design: pine green #1F5C3F, cream #FFF7E8, sunny yellow #FFC93C. headlines big and chunky and rounded. normal text clean and simple. REAL photos of dogs outside only, no clip art, no cartoon dogs, no stock photos of dogs that aren't ours. if we don't have the photo leave a space for it.

my story: I started this after my own dog Juniper died last year. I was walking past all these houses with dogs barking at the window all day. I thought, I can fix that, one street at a time. I don't love telling this story in posts but it's fine on the website "about" bit, short.

rules I always forget:
- every post ends with the free first walk line
- double check the price against the price list, I got it wrong once and a customer held me to it
- never promise we're "insured" or "certified" in writing, we're getting insurance sorted but it's not done yet
- never post a photo of a client's house number

social: instagram mostly. 1 post a week, on sunday evening. it's a little story from the week + the free walk line. stories during the week when something funny happens.

stuff that isn't done: website (I have nothing, just the instagram), booking (people just text me), a proper price page.
