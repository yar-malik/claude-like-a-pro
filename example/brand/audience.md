# Audience

Mostly three kinds of customer:

1. **Office people.** Leave at 8, back at 6. They feel guilty. They want a photo.
2. **New parents.** Tired. The dog is suddenly getting less attention, and they know it.
3. **Older owners.** Their knees can't do the long loop anymore. They want someone they can trust with a key.

The question underneath for all of them: is my dog safe and happy while I'm not there?
