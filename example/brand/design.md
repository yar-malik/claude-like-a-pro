# Design

## Colours
- Pine green `#1F5C3F`
- Cream `#FFF7E8`
- Sunny yellow `#FFC93C`

## Type
- Headlines: big, chunky, rounded.
- Body text: clean and simple.

## Photos
- Real photos of our dogs, outside, only.
- No clip art, no cartoon dogs, no stock photos of dogs that aren't ours.
- If we don't have the photo yet, leave a space for it. Don't fill it with something else.
