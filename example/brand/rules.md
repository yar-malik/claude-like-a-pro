# Rules

## Before anything goes out
- Every post ends with the free first walk line (details in brand/offer.md).
- Check every price against brand/offer.md. Sam got one wrong once and a customer held them to it.
- Never say in writing that we're "insured" or "certified". Insurance is being sorted but isn't done yet.
- Never post a photo that shows a client's house number.

## Posting
- Instagram is the main channel.
- One post a week, Sunday evening: a little story from the week, plus the free walk line.
- Stories during the week when something funny happens.
