# Voice

How Sam talks, in every post, text and caption.

- Short sentences.
- Sound like a neighbour, not a company.
- Use the dog's name whenever you know it.
- One joke at most. People want to know their dog is ok, not watch a comedy show.
- Say "walk", never "adventure".
- Never write "furry friends" or "fur babies".
- No more than one exclamation mark per message.
- Sign texts "Sam", not "the team".
