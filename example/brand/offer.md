# Offer

This is the only place prices live. Prices change sometimes, so update them here and nowhere else.

## Prices
- Solo walk, 30 min: $25
- Pack walk, 60 min, max 4 dogs: $35
- Puppy check-in, 15 min: $15
- First walk is always free. Just text 555-0142.
- 10% off when you book 5 walks in one week.

## Not offered
- No overnight sitting. People ask all the time; the answer is no. Recommend Rosa on Cedar for that.

## Where
- Pine Street and the four streets around it: Maple, Cedar, Holloway, and Grant Ave up to the park.
- Not going wider than that. The whole point is being local and able to be there in 5 minutes.
- Rainy days: we use the covered path behind the library.

## How people book
- By text, to the number above. There's no booking system.
- No website yet (only Instagram) and no proper price page yet.
