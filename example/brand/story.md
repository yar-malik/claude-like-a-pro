# Story

## Who we are
Pine Street Dog Walks is Sam. Sam's cousin Priya sometimes covers Fridays.

## Why it started
Sam started it after their own dog, Juniper, died last year. Walking past all the houses with dogs barking at the window all day, Sam thought: I can fix that, one street at a time.

Where to tell it: fine on the website's About section, kept short. Sam doesn't love telling it in posts, so leave it out of social.
